Supervisor User Guide
FOR SUPERVISORS
NMWC Customer Master · v1.0 · 2026-05

Welcome

You are the gate between field-collected data and the master record. Every salesman submission lands in your queue. Your job is to approve good ones quickly, reject bad ones with a clear reason, and use bulk actions when a batch is uniformly good. This guide walks you through every screen.

1. Logging in

  1. Open https://nmwc-cm.vercel.app on your phone or laptop.
  2. Sign in with your supervisor username and password.
  3. After signing in you land on the Approvals page automatically.

2. Open the approval queue

  1. Tap Approvals in the menu.
  2. You see every pending submission from salesmen who report to you, oldest first.
  3. Each row shows: customer name, NMWC code, number of changed fields, who submitted, and how long ago.
  4. Age tags: green < 24h, amber 1-3 days, red > 3 days. Clear the reds first.

3. Review one edit (Before / After diff)

  1. Tap any row to open the edit detail page.
  2. You see a Before / After diff for every field that changed.
  3. Compare the values. Anything wrong, missing, or suspicious?
  4. If a photo changed, tap it to view full size.
  5. Tap Open profile to see the full customer record (other fields, branches, history).

4. Approve, reject, or send back

✓ ApproveThe change is correct. Tap the green Approve button. A confirmation popup asks you to confirm. The change goes live on the master immediately.
✗ Reject (send back for correction)Something needs fixing. Tap the red Reject button. Pick a category (Bad photo, Wrong GPS, Missing field, Wrong info, Other), pick a canned reason from the suggestion pills, or type your own (5+ letters). The salesman sees this exactly and can re-submit after fixing.
Don't approve your own workThe system blocks you from approving your own submissions. If you ever submitted as a salesman first, that edit must go to another supervisor or to your manager.

5. Bulk approve / bulk reject

When a batch is uniformly good (or uniformly bad).

  1. On the queue page, tick the checkbox next to each edit you want to handle. Use Select all to grab everything on the page.
  2. A sticky bar appears at the bottom with Approve N and Reject N buttons.
  3. Bulk approve runs each one in its own transaction. If a few fail (concurrent edits, missing fields), the others still go through. You see a clear list of which ones need attention.
  4. Bulk reject sends the same category and reason to every selected edit. Use only when the issue is truly the same on all of them.
Tip — when not to bulk-approveIf even one edit looks suspicious, open it and review individually. Bulk-approve is for trust + speed, not for skipping the check.

6. Reject categories — when to use which

CategoryUse it when…
Bad photoPhoto is blurry, taken from too far, or doesn't show what it should (e.g. CR photo where the writing is unreadable).
Wrong GPSGPS coordinates point to the wrong place — e.g. salesman captured GPS at the office instead of the shop.
Missing fieldA required field is empty or has a placeholder.
Wrong infoA field has data that doesn't match the photo or what we know about the shop.
OtherAnything else — type a clear reason.

7. Common situations

Message you seeWhat it means
"Edit was just decided by another reviewer"Another supervisor or manager approved/rejected before you. Refresh the queue.
"This value conflicts with an existing record"Two salesmen submitted the same phone or CR number. Reject one and ask them to verify.
"Required fields are now missing"Salesman deleted a required field (e.g. CR photo) between submitting and your approving. Reject — they need to re-upload at the shop.
"You are not authorized to act on this edit"The customer is no longer in your team's scope (route reassigned, customer merged). Forward to your manager.
