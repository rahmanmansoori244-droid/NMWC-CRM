Welcome

This is your daily companion. The NMWC Customer Master app helps you keep every shop on your route up-to-date — directly from the field. This guide walks you through every action you will need: logging in, finding a shop, fixing missing info, taking photos, capturing GPS, submitting for approval, and handling shop closures or reopenings.

1. Logging in

You only do this the first time, then once a day or week.

  1. On your phone, open https://nmwc-cm.vercel.app. Save it as a bookmark on your home screen.
  2. Type your Username. Your manager gives you this on day one.
  3. Type your Password. Tap the blue Sign in button.
  4. First time? The app may ask you to set a new password. This is for your safety — pick something only you know.
Forgot your password?Don't guess more than 5 times — the system will lock you out for one minute. Just message your manager — they reset it in 30 seconds.

2. Find the shop you are visiting

Open the customer in two taps.

  1. Tap Today in the menu. You see the shops on your route scheduled today.
  2. If the shop is not on today's list, tap Customers and search by name or NMWC code.
  3. Tap any row to open that shop's profile.
What's a "branch"?A "customer" is the legal entity (company name on the CR). A "branch" is the physical shop you visit. Most shops have one branch ("Main"). Big chains have several.

3. Update the shop info

Fill in what is missing. Required fields are marked with *.

  1. On the customer profile, tap the blue Enrich button at the top right.
  2. Identity section: legal name, NMWC code, CR number, channel, sub-channel, contact person, primary phone.
  3. Phone must be a valid Oman number, e.g. +96891234567.
  4. Channel says what type of shop it is — pick the closest match (General Trade, HORECA, Modern Trade, etc.).

4. Take the photos

Always take fresh photos at the shop today. Old gallery photos are rejected. The shop-front photo is required to submit; the others raise the completeness score.

  1. Tap an empty photo slot. Your camera opens.
  2. Shop front (required) — clearly shows the entrance. For a home-delivery customer, the building entrance.
  3. Signboard — the shop's sign with its name, when the shop has one.
  4. CR document — the legal commercial registration paper, when the customer is a registered business.
  5. The app uploads automatically. If your network is weak, it retries on its own. If still failing, tap Retry upload — your photo is saved, no need to retake.
Take photos at the shop, not from the galleryPhotos older than 24 hours are rejected. Supervisors can see when each photo was taken. Taking it on the spot is the only way.

5. Capture the GPS location

  1. Tap the Capture GPS button when you're standing at the shop entrance.
  2. Your phone asks permission — tap Allow.
  3. If GPS doesn't work (indoors, weak signal), tap Enter coordinates manually. Tap the map at the shop's location, or type the lat/lng. Add a short reason so the supervisor sees it's manual.

6. Submit for approval

  1. Scroll to the bottom — you see a sticky bar with two buttons.
  2. Tap the blue Submit for approval button (left).
  3. You'll see a confirmation. The shop's status becomes Pending review until your supervisor decides.
  4. If you're interrupted, tap Save draft instead — it stays on your phone for 7 days.

7. Mark a shop as permanently closed

Use this only if the shop has truly shut down (gone out of business, moved, etc.).

  1. On the customer profile, tap the red Mark closed button on the branch tile.
  2. Take a fresh photo showing it's clearly closed (shutters down, sign removed, empty).
  3. Type a short reason — at least 5 letters. e.g. "Shop has shut down, signage removed."
  4. Tap Submit closure. Your supervisor reviews it.
Be sure before you mark itA blurry photo or one taken from far away will be rejected. Show the closed entrance clearly.

8. Request reactivation of a closed shop

Use this when a previously closed shop has reopened.

  1. Open the closed shop's profile. The branch shows a Closed tag.
  2. Tap the green Request reactivation button.
  3. Take a fresh photo showing the shop is open and operating today.
  4. Type a reason and submit. The manager (not your supervisor) approves reactivations.
Why a fresh photo?The system checks the photo was taken AFTER the shop was marked closed. This prevents anyone from using an old photo to fake a reopening.

9. Common situations

SituationWhat to do
Photo upload keeps failingMove outside or near a window. Wait a few seconds. The app retries automatically. If still stuck, tap "Retry upload" — your photo is saved.
GPS won't captureTap "Enter coordinates manually" inside the GPS box. Tap the map or type the numbers. Add a short reason.
"This customer is not on your route"You can only enrich shops on your assigned route. Ask your manager to reassign it if needed.
My submission was rejectedRead the supervisor's reason. The shop appears in your "Needs correction" list. Open it, fix what they asked, and re-submit.
I'm mid-form and need to leaveTap "Save draft". You can come back within 7 days and finish.
The signal dropped while I was submittingThe app says, beside the button, whether your submit arrived. Stay on the page and tap "Try again" when you have signal. If it had already arrived you see "Already received" — it is never sent twice.

10. Quick reference card

Keep this page handy in your first weeks.

Buttons you'll use

Enrich (blue, top-right)Open the form to update the shop.
Save draftSave your work without sending. Stays 7 days.
Submit for approvalSend your changes to your supervisor.
Mark closed (red)Tell the system this shop has permanently closed.
Request reactivation (green)A closed shop has reopened — send to manager.
Retry uploadRe-send a failed photo without retaking it.
Sign outEnd your session. Always sign out on shared devices.

Status tags you'll see

Active (green)Shop is operating normally.
Closed (red)Shop has shut down. No active orders.
Pending reviewYou submitted, supervisor hasn't decided yet.
ApprovedYour last submission was approved — change is live.
Needs correctionSupervisor sent it back. Open and fix.

Who to contact

Forgot password / locked outYour manager — they reset in 30 seconds.
Photo or GPS not workingTry the manual fallback. Then your supervisor.
Wrong info in the masterSubmit an enrichment with the correction.
