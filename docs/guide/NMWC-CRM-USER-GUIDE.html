Welcome

The NMWC Customer Master app helps every team keep our shop information clean, complete, and up-to-date — directly from the field. This guide shows you exactly what to do, depending on your role.

Who does what

SALESMAN

You visit the shop

You go to the shop, fix the missing customer info, take photos of the shop and the CR, and submit. If a shop has closed, you mark it closed. If one reopens, you request reactivation.

SUPERVISOR

You review the work

You see all submissions from your team in one queue. You approve good ones, reject bad ones with a clear reason, or send them back for correction. Bulk-approve when you're confident.

MANAGER

You own the region

You review reactivations (bringing closed shops back), reset passwords for your team, manage routes and users, and watch overall completeness.

STEWARD (head office)

You own the master

You bulk-import customer lists from the ERP, merge duplicates, and export the cleaned master back to the ERP. Not part of the daily field cycle.

1. Logging in

Everybody — salesman, supervisor, manager — uses the same way to log in.

Sign-in screen
  1. Open this link on your phone or laptop:
    https://nmwc-cm.vercel.app
  2. Type your Username (your manager gives this to you).
  3. Type your Password.
  4. Tap the blue Sign in button.
First time logging in? The app may ask you to set a new password before you can do anything else. This is for your security — a temporary password isn't a permanent password.
Forgot your password? Don't try to guess it more than 5 times — the system will lock you out for a minute. Just message your Manager and they will reset it for you in seconds.

Recommended

  • Save the link as a bookmark on your home screen so you can open it with one tap.
  • Don't share your password with anyone, including a colleague — every action is logged in your name.
FOR SALESMEN

2. Visit a shop and update its info

This is your daily job. You visit a shop, find it in the app, fix what's missing, take photos, and submit. Your supervisor approves, and the master is updated.

Step 1 — Find the shop you're visiting

"Today" — your route for the day
  1. Tap Today in the menu (or go to the home screen). You'll see the customers on your route who have visits scheduled today.
  2. If the shop you're at isn't on today's list, tap Customers to search the full list.
  3. Tap a customer to open their profile.
  1. The Customers page shows all the shops on your route. Use the search at the top to find one quickly by name or NMWC code.
  2. Each row shows the shop's completeness — the green ring tells you how much info is missing.
Search and pick a customer

Step 2 — Open the customer profile

A shop's profile screen
  1. You'll see the basic info we already have on this shop: name, NMWC code, phone, channel, contact, etc.
  2. Empty fields and a low completeness score tell you what to fill in.
  3. Scroll down to see the branches of this customer. Most shops have one branch ("Main") but big chains may have several.
  4. Tap the blue Enrich button at the top right to start updating.
What's a branch? A "customer" is the legal entity (the company name on the CR). A "branch" is the physical shop you visit. One customer can have many branches if it's a chain.

Step 3 — Fill in what's missing

The enrichment form — top section
  1. Identity section: legal name, NMWC code, CR number, channel and sub-channel, contact person, primary phone.
  2. Required fields are marked with *. The phone number must be a valid Oman number (e.g. +96891234567).
  3. Channel tells the company what kind of shop this is: General Trade, HORECA, Modern Trade, etc. Pick the closest match.
  4. Tap into a field to edit it. Empty fields show a placeholder text — type what you see at the shop.

Step 4 — Capture photos

  1. Three photos are required:
    • Shop front — clearly shows the entrance.
    • Signboard — the shop's sign. Helps verify the legal name.
    • CR document — the commercial registration paper.
  2. Tap an empty photo slot. Your phone's camera opens.
  3. Take the photo. The app uploads it automatically.
  4. If the upload fails (weak network), the app retries by itself. If after a few retries it still doesn't work, tap Retry upload — your photo is saved, you don't need to retake it.
Photo capture slots
Take fresh photos at the shop Old photos from your gallery don't count. The app rejects photos older than 24 hours, and supervisors can see when each photo was taken. Take it now, on the spot.

Step 5 — Capture the GPS location

  1. The form has a Capture GPS button. Tap it once you're at the shop's entrance.
  2. Your phone will ask permission to share your location — tap Allow.
  3. The app reads the latitude / longitude and stamps the time of capture.
  4. If the GPS doesn't work (you're indoors, or signal is weak), tap Enter coordinates manually and either tap on a map or type the numbers — but you'll need to write a short reason so your supervisor sees this is a special case.

Step 6 — Submit

Sticky submit bar at the bottom
  1. Scroll to the bottom. You'll see a sticky bar with two buttons.
  2. Submit for approval (left, blue) — sends the changes to your supervisor for review.
  3. Save draft (right) — saves what you've typed so far. Useful if a customer interrupts you. You can come back and finish later. Drafts stay on your phone for 7 days.
  4. After submitting, you see a confirmation. The shop's status becomes Pending review until the supervisor decides.
What happens after I submit? Your supervisor sees your submission in their queue. If they approve, the master is updated immediately. If they reject, you get a clear reason, and the shop appears in your Needs correction list. Fix what they asked for and re-submit.
FOR SALESMEN

3. Mark a shop closed

If a shop has shut down (gone out of business, moved, etc.), tell the system. This is different from a temporary closure — be sure before you mark it.

  1. Open the customer profile for the shop.
  2. On the branch tile, tap the red Mark closed button.
  3. A small form opens. Take a fresh photo of the shop showing it's clearly closed (shutters down, signage removed, empty premises).
  4. Type a reason in your own words — e.g. "Shop has shut down, signage removed, owner moved to another location." (5 characters minimum.)
  5. Tap Submit closure. Your supervisor will review it.
Marking a shop closed is reversible — but evidence matters The supervisor needs to see proof. A photo from far away or a blurry one will be rejected. Show the closed entrance clearly.

What if it was a mistake?

If you marked the wrong shop closed, contact your supervisor immediately. They can reject the request before it takes effect. Once approved, the shop status is "CLOSED" until a manager re-activates it.

FOR SALESMEN

4. Request reactivation of a closed shop

If a previously closed shop has reopened (same location, may be same or new owner), use this to bring it back into the active master.

  1. Open the customer profile of the closed shop. The branch will show a Closed tag.
  2. Tap the green Request reactivation button.
  3. Take a fresh photo of the shop today, clearly showing it's open and operating.
  4. Type a reason — e.g. "Shop has reopened, signage back up, owner returned, accepts deliveries again."
  5. Tap Request reactivation.
  6. Important: the manager approves reactivations (not the supervisor), because bringing a shop back is more sensitive than a routine update.
Why a fresh photo? The system checks that your photo was taken AFTER the shop was marked closed. This stops anyone from using an old "before-closure" photo to fake a reactivation.
FOR SUPERVISORS

5. Review your team's work

Every submission your team makes lands in your approvals queue. Approve good ones, reject bad ones with a clear reason. You can also bulk-approve when you trust the batch.

Step 1 — Open the queue

Your supervisor queue
  1. Tap Approvals in the menu.
  2. You'll see every pending submission from salesmen who report to you, oldest first.
  3. Each row shows: customer name, NMWC code, number of changes, who submitted, and how long ago.
  4. The colored age tag tells you urgency: green < 24 hours, amber 1–3 days, red over 3 days. Try to clear reds first.

Step 2 — Review one edit at a time

  1. Tap a row to open it. You'll see a Before / After diff for every field that changed.
  2. Compare the values. Anything wrong, missing, or suspicious?
  3. If a photo changed, tap it to view full size.
  4. Tap Open profile to see the full customer record (other fields, branches, history).
Before / After diff view

Step 3 — Approve, reject, or send back

✓ Approve The change is correct and complete. Tap the green Approve button. A confirmation popup asks you to confirm. The change goes live on the master immediately.
✗ Reject (send back for correction) Something needs fixing. Tap the red Reject button. Pick a category (Bad photo, Wrong GPS, Missing field, Wrong info, Other), then either pick a canned reason from the suggestions, or type your own (5 characters minimum). The salesman will see this exactly and can re-submit after fixing.
Open profile (just looking) Click "Open profile" if you want to look at the full customer record without making a decision.

Bulk approve / bulk reject

  1. On the queue page, tick the checkbox next to each edit you want to handle.
  2. Use Select all at the top to grab everything on the page.
  3. A sticky bar appears at the bottom with Approve N and Reject N buttons.
  4. Bulk-approve runs each one in its own transaction — if a few fail (concurrent edits, missing fields), the others still go through and you'll see a clear list of which ones need attention.
  5. Bulk-reject sends the same category and reason to every selected edit. Use only when the issue is truly the same on all of them.
Don't approve your own work The system blocks you from approving your own submissions. If you accidentally end up reviewing an edit you submitted, send it to another supervisor or to your manager.
FOR MANAGERS

6. Manager — Reactivations and team management

As a manager, you handle the high-trust decisions: approving reopenings of closed shops, resetting team passwords, and managing routes.

Step 1 — See your dashboard

Manager home — at a glance
  1. The Dashboard shows totals for your region: active customers, pending approvals, reactivation requests, recent activity.
  2. Use it as a daily morning check.

Step 2 — Review reactivations

  1. Tap Reactivations in the menu.
  2. You see all the closed shops your salesmen have asked to reopen.
  3. Each row shows the salesman's reason and the photo they captured at the shop today.
  4. Tap the photo to see it full size and confirm the shop is genuinely open.
  5. Tap ✓ Reactivate if you're satisfied — the branch goes back to ACTIVE.
  6. Tap Keep closed if you don't agree — the salesman gets a notification.
Reactivation queue

Step 3 — Manage users

Users — your team
  1. Tap Users in the menu.
  2. You see every user in your region: salesmen, supervisors, anyone reporting up to you.
  3. Common actions:
    • Reset password — for a salesman who forgot theirs. Set a temporary password; the system forces them to change it on next login.
    • Disable — when a salesman leaves the company. They can no longer log in. Their history stays in audit logs.
    • Reassign route — if a salesman switches routes.

Other manager actions

Routes & regionsAdd a new route, mark one inactive, see which salesman owns each route.
Audit logSee every action by every user, with timestamp, IP, and old/new values. Use when investigating something unusual.
Approvals (override)You see your region's full approval queue. You can approve edits if a supervisor is unavailable.
Direct-write power — use sparingly As a manager you can edit customer fields directly without going through approval. This is logged with FORCE_OVERRIDE in the audit. Use only for emergencies — it bypasses the team's training cycle.

7. Common situations and answers

For salesmen

"My photo upload keeps failing"Move outside, near a window, or wait 30 seconds for signal. The app retries automatically. If it still doesn't work, tap "Retry upload" — your photo is saved, you don't need to retake it.
"GPS won't capture"Tap "Enter coordinates manually" inside the GPS section, then tap on the map or type the lat/lng. Add a short reason. Your supervisor will see it's manual and may verify it themselves.
"This customer isn't on my route"You can only enrich customers on your assigned route. Ask your manager to reassign it if you really did visit it.
"My submission was rejected"Read the reason your supervisor gave. The shop is now in your "Needs correction" list. Open it, fix what they asked for, take a fresh photo if needed, and submit again.
"I'm in the middle of a form and need to leave"Tap "Save draft" — you can come back within 7 days and finish where you left off.

For supervisors

"Edit was just decided by another reviewer"This means another supervisor (or a manager) approved or rejected it before you did. Refresh the queue.
"This value conflicts with an existing record"Two salesmen submitted the same phone or CR number. Reject one and ask them to verify which customer is correct.
"Required fields are missing"The salesman deleted a required field (like the CR photo) between submitting and your approving. Reject and ask them to re-upload at the shop.

For managers

"Photo was captured before the last status change"The salesman tried to use an old photo for a reactivation. Reject — they need to take a fresh photo at the shop today.
"My team can't log in"Check the audit log for "LOGIN_FAIL" entries. Usually it's a typed password (the system locks them out for 1 minute after 5 wrong tries).
